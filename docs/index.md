# Privacy Policy for PDF Master (ScanPro AI)

**Effective Date:** September 11, 2026

Thank you for choosing to use PDF Master ("the App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## 1. Data Collection and Usage
Our app is designed with a strong focus on privacy. Most operations occur entirely on your local device.

### a. Camera and Photo Library
The App requires access to your device's camera and photo gallery to capture images and convert them into PDF documents. The images and the resulting PDFs are processed and stored locally on your device. We do not upload, store, or view your personal documents on any external servers.

### b. Local Storage
All generated and edited PDF files are saved directly to your device's local storage. We have no access to these files.

### c. Biometric Authentication (App Lock)
If you enable the "App Lock" feature, the App uses your device's native biometric authentication (fingerprint or face recognition) to secure access. Your biometric data never leaves your device and is handled entirely by your device's operating system. We do not collect or store this data.

### d. AI Chat Feature
The App includes an AI-powered chat feature that allows you to summarize and ask questions about your PDF documents. When you use this feature, the text extracted from the document and your chat messages are sent securely to the **OpenAI API** to generate responses.
* You are required to provide your own OpenAI API key. 
* OpenAI's data handling is governed by their own Privacy Policy and API terms (data sent via the API is generally not used to train OpenAI models by default). 
* We do not intercept, store, or log your API key, document contents, or chat history on any of our own servers.
